About

I'm Nico Pfiffner, a PhD researcher and developer with expertise in participant-centered data collection methods.

I created the Data Donation Module, an open-source data donation collection tool, and wrote my dissertation on the opportunities, challenges, and applications of data donation for studying digital media use and other phenomena. My research is published in peer-reviewed journals.

I work with universities and research teams to build reliable, ethical data collection infrastructure — bridging academic methodology and working software.

Available for project-based engagements.
